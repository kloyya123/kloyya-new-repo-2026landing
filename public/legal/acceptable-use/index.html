Acceptable Use & AI Policy

Kloyya is designed to help people understand information and make better-informed decisions. Users must use Kloyya responsibly and lawfully.

Prohibited Activities

You may not use Kloyya to:

  • commit fraud;
  • impersonate individuals;
  • distribute malware;
  • conduct unauthorized surveillance;
  • unlawfully obtain personal information;
  • facilitate cyberattacks;
  • evade security controls;
  • generate deceptive content for unlawful purposes;
  • harass or threaten people;
  • exploit minors;
  • violate intellectual-property rights;
  • conduct unlawful discrimination;
  • facilitate illegal financial activity;
  • circumvent sanctions or applicable laws;
  • interfere with Kloyya infrastructure.

Human Oversight

  • Kloyya’s outputs should be reviewed by a human before being used for consequential decisions.
  • Kloyya should not be treated as an autonomous authority.
  • Users remain responsible for decisions made using Kloyya.

Accuracy

AI systems can make mistakes. Kloyya may:

  • misunderstand context;
  • retrieve incomplete information;
  • generate inaccurate conclusions;
  • make incorrect recommendations;
  • fail to identify relevant information;
  • misinterpret documents.

Where possible, Kloyya may provide evidence, sources, context, or confidence indicators to help users evaluate outputs.

Confidential Information

You should only connect accounts and upload information that you are authorized to provide to Kloyya.

Do not upload confidential information belonging to another person or organization unless you have appropriate authorization.

Automated Actions

Where Kloyya provides actions or automation capabilities, users remain responsible for configuring appropriate permissions.

Kloyya may require confirmation before performing certain sensitive actions.

Model Providers

Kloyya may use different AI models and infrastructure providers.

The underlying model may change as Kloyya improves its technology.
