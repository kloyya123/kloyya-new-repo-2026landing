Cookie Policy

Kloyya uses cookies and similar technologies to operate, secure, analyze, and improve its Services.

1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a website.

Similar technologies may include pixels, local storage, SDKs, and device identifiers.

2. Types of Cookies

Strictly Necessary Cookies

These are required for functions such as:

  • authentication;
  • account security;
  • session management;
  • fraud prevention;
  • basic website operation.

These generally cannot be disabled through ordinary cookie controls where they are essential to provide the service.

Functional Cookies

These help remember preferences and settings.

Analytics Cookies

These help us understand:

  • how visitors use Kloyya;
  • which pages are used;
  • performance;
  • errors;
  • general product usage.

Marketing Cookies

Where used, these may help measure advertising or marketing effectiveness.

Marketing cookies will be used according to applicable consent requirements.

3. Your Choices

Depending on your location, you may be able to:

  • accept cookies;
  • reject optional cookies;
  • change cookie preferences;
  • withdraw consent.

Where applicable law requires consent, Kloyya will request consent before placing non-essential cookies.

4. Third-Party Technologies

Kloyya may use third-party providers for analytics, security, payments, communications, and other functionality.

These providers may place or access cookies or similar technologies according to their own policies and our configuration.

5. Browser Controls

You can also control cookies through your browser.

Disabling certain cookies may cause portions of Kloyya to stop functioning properly.
