Payments, Refund & Cancellation Policy

This Policy explains how Kloyya charges for paid plans, how subscriptions renew and cancel, and when refunds are available. It forms part of the Kloyya Terms of Service.

1. Pricing

  • Kloyya may offer free and paid plans.
  • Current pricing will be displayed on the Kloyya website or inside the application before purchase.
  • All prices will identify the applicable billing interval.
  • Applicable taxes may be added where required.

2. Billing

  • Paid subscriptions are billed in advance on a monthly or annual basis, depending on the plan selected.
  • Your payment method will be charged at the beginning of each billing period.
  • For recurring subscriptions, the subscription will automatically renew unless cancelled before the next renewal date.

3. Recurring Charges

Before subscribing, Kloyya will provide information about:

  • subscription price;
  • billing frequency;
  • applicable trial or promotional period;
  • renewal terms;
  • cancellation method.

By completing the purchase, you authorize the applicable recurring charges.

4. Free Trials

  • If a free trial is offered, the trial period and conversion terms will be clearly displayed before signup.
  • Unless you cancel before the trial ends, the subscription may convert into a paid plan according to the terms displayed at signup.

5. Cancellation

  • You may cancel your subscription at any time through the available account cancellation controls.
  • Kloyya will not require you to provide a reason for cancellation unless required for a specific legal or administrative purpose.
  • Cancellation stops future renewal charges.
  • Unless otherwise required by applicable law, cancellation does not automatically create a refund for the current billing period.

6. Refunds

Except where required by applicable law, subscription fees are generally non-refundable after a billing period begins.

However, Kloyya may issue refunds or credits at its discretion in circumstances including:

  • duplicate charges;
  • billing errors;
  • unauthorized charges;
  • substantial service failures;
  • accidental purchases where reasonably demonstrated;
  • other circumstances determined appropriate by Kloyya.

7. European Union and European Economic Area Consumers

  • Nothing in this policy limits mandatory consumer rights.
  • Where applicable, consumers may have a statutory right of withdrawal from distance contracts.
  • Certain exceptions may apply, including certain digital services after performance begins with the consumer’s required consent and acknowledgement, and certain services supplied for specific dates.
  • Where a statutory refund is required, Kloyya will process it within the period required by applicable law.

8. United Kingdom

  • Where applicable, consumers may have statutory cancellation, refund, and consumer rights under UK law.
  • Nothing in this Policy removes mandatory statutory rights.

9. United States

  • U.S. consumers retain all rights provided by applicable federal and state law.
  • Kloyya will provide required subscription disclosures and cancellation mechanisms.
  • Where state law provides additional refund or cancellation rights, those rights will apply.

10. Africa and Other Jurisdictions

  • Customers in African countries and other jurisdictions retain mandatory rights provided by applicable consumer-protection and electronic-commerce laws.
  • Where local law requires a refund, cancellation right, cooling-off period, or other consumer protection, Kloyya will comply with that requirement.

11. Chargebacks

If you believe a payment was unauthorized or incorrect, contact us first:

Email: contactsupport@kloyya.com

Nothing in this Policy prevents you from exercising rights provided by your payment provider or applicable law.

12. Refund Requests

Refund requests should include:

  • account email;
  • transaction date;
  • amount charged;
  • reason for the request;
  • transaction or invoice reference where available.

Send requests to contactsupport@kloyya.com. We generally aim to respond within 5 business days.

13. Currency

  • Kloyya may display prices in USD or other supported currencies.
  • Your bank or payment provider may apply currency-conversion fees or exchange rates.
  • Kloyya is not responsible for fees imposed by your bank or card provider.

14. Payment Providers

  • Payments may be processed by third-party payment providers.
  • Kloyya does not normally store complete payment-card numbers.
  • Payment processing is subject to the applicable provider’s terms and privacy policy.

15. Failed Payments

If a payment fails, Kloyya may:

  • notify you;
  • retry the payment;
  • restrict paid features;
  • suspend the subscription; or
  • terminate the subscription after reasonable notice.

16. Taxes

  • Where legally required, Kloyya may collect applicable sales tax, VAT, GST, or similar taxes.
  • You are responsible for taxes that are legally imposed on you.
